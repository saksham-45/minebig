About Us

Our story — 25 years in the making.

Long before it was ever about the tech…

For 25 years, MineBig has been a name people trusted — passed along by word of mouth, community to community, long before it was ever about the tech.

Now, under transformed guidance and a higher intent to serve hope with care, MineBig has been reborn — more refined, in a fresh new attire. This is MineBig's Version 2.0.

Every Sunday at 12 PM, we run a live draw — simple and open for everyone to watch. Behind every number is a verified agent, with you from the moment you pick your 6 numbers to the moment a winner is handed their prize, face to face. No confusing apps, no hidden steps — just people, numbers, and a real chance.

Same trust. Same dreams. Now, better than ever.
