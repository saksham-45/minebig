What is MineBig.com?

📺 Watch the draw live

Every Sunday at 12 PM, a live 15-minute draw. Simple, open, and everyone can watch. Outside the live window, a countdown ticks down to the next draw.

🔢 Pick your 6 numbers

Choose any 6 natural numbers. Check live whether each number is still available — and get smart suggestions when one is taken. Your code is locked the moment you buy it.

🤝 Everything through your agent

Buy your ticket, receive your receipt, and collect your prize — all face to face with a verified local agent. No apps, no hidden steps. Just people, numbers, and a real chance.

🏆 7 prize tiers weekly

1st, 2nd, 3rd, a Special Prize, and three Consolation prizes — every single Sunday, all in RM.

📜 Transparent winners archive

Every winning number published — the last 3 months in full, plus a lifetime record of which numbers have won over the years.

🔎 Check any code

Enter your 6-number code and see if it won — or check whether a number is still available before you buy. No login needed.

How the draw works

1

Pick your 6 numbers

Any natural numbers that are still available this week. Check availability live — if a number is taken, the site suggests available alternatives.

2

Your agent locks your code

Buy through a verified agent. The moment you buy, that combination is created and blocked — no one else can sell it until Sunday.

3

Watch the live draw — Sunday 12 PM

Every draw is live and open for everyone to watch. Simple, transparent, and announced right here on the website.

4

Winners are published & contacted

Results go up on the site and the agent portal. Your agent reaches out and hands over your prize, face to face.

Where to claim

🎟️ Ticket & receipt

Your agent sells the ticket and hands you the receipt in person. Nothing is delivered digitally — it's all human, all trusted.

🏆 Prize handover

Win? Your agent confirms your code and hands over your prize face to face. Every tier — from 1st to Consolation 3 — is delivered with care.

🚫 No online payments, ever

This website never takes payments. It informs, connects, and publishes results — the agent handles the rest, offline.
