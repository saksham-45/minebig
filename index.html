What is MineBig.com?

► Watch the draw live

Every Sunday at 12 PM, a live 15-minute draw. Simple, open, and everyone can watch. Outside the live window, a countdown ticks down to the next draw.

# Two games to play

MineBig 4D — pick your lucky 4 digits. MineBig 6D — six digits for a bigger shot at the jackpot. Check availability live before you buy.

↔ Everything through your agent

Buy your ticket, receive your receipt, and collect your prize — all face to face with a verified local agent. No payments happen on this website.

★ 5 prize tiers weekly

1st, 2nd, 3rd, Special and Consolation prizes — every single Sunday, for both games.

▤ Transparent results archive

Every winning number published — a browsable timeline of past draws with winner locations.

? Check any number

Check whether a number is still available before you buy, and check your ticket status. No login needed.

How the draw works

1

Pick your numbers

Choose a 4-digit or 6-digit number that is still available this week. Check availability live — the site tells you instantly.

2

Connect with your agent

Your agent completes the purchase face to face. No payment happens on the website — the agent handles the transaction.

3

Watch the live draw — Sunday 12 PM

Every draw is live and open for everyone to watch. Simple, transparent, and announced right here on the website.

4

Collect your prize face to face

Winners are published on this site. Your agent contacts you and hands over your prize in person — winner names stay private, only the city and country are shown.

Where to claim

↔ Through your agent

Your ticket, your receipt and your prize all come from the same verified agent who sold you the ticket. No apps, no hidden steps.

★ Winner privacy

Results show the winning number and the winner's city and country — never their name. Identity stays between the winner and their agent.
