Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the draw, tickets, and how your agent takes care of the rest.

When is the draw?

Every Sunday at 12:00 PM (your local time). The live event runs for 15 minutes — watch it on the Upcoming Events page. When it ends, the countdown to next Sunday starts automatically.

How do I buy a ticket?

Through a verified agent. Use the Contact an agent form — an agent will call you, take your 6 numbers, and sell you the ticket face to face. There are no online payments on this site; everything is handled by your agent in person.

What numbers can I pick?

Any 6 natural numbers (1, 2, 3…) — as long as each one is still available that week. You can check availability live on the Check Status page, and the site suggests alternatives for numbers that are taken.

Can two people have the same code?

No. The moment a code is bought it is locked — no other agent can sell that combination until the week ends. Every Sunday at 12 PM all codes reset and the pool opens fresh.

What prizes are on offer?

Seven tiers every draw: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, Special Prize, and Consolation 1, 2 & 3. Prize amounts are announced with each draw.

How do I know if I won?

Three ways: watch the live draw, check your code on the Check Status page, or see the Winning Numbers page. Winners are also announced on the agent portal, and your agent will contact you personally.

How do I collect a prize?

Face to face, from your agent — the same person who sold you the ticket. Your agent confirms your code, hands over the prize, and that's it. No apps, no hidden steps.

Do I need an account to play?

No. Checking status, availability and results works without an account. A free Player Login lets you save and track your codes in one place.

Is there an app?

Not yet — the website works on any phone or computer. No downloads needed.

Is MineBig safe and legitimate?

MineBig is a 25-year-old name, reborn as Version 2.0. The draw is live and open for everyone to watch, results are published transparently, and every sale and prize goes through verified agents with a permanent record. Please also read our Play Responsibly page.
