Frequently asked questions

Getting started, buying numbers, draws, prizes and support — everything you need to know.

Getting Started

What is MineBig?

MineBig is a 25-year-old name, reborn as Version 2.0 — a draw/lottery brand where the website informs and connects, while every real transaction (ticket sale, receipt, prize) happens face to face through a verified agent.

Do I need an account to play?

No. Checking availability, results and status works without an account. A free Player Login lets you save and track your numbers in one place.

Is there an app?

Not yet — the website works on any phone or computer. No downloads needed.

Try Your Luck / Buying Numbers

How do I check if my number is available?

Visit the Try Your Luck page, choose MineBig 4D or MineBig 6D, enter your number and tap Check Availability. If it's available, add it to your selection.

How do I connect with an agent?

On the Try Your Luck page, tap Connect to an Agent with your selection — or use the Connect with an Agent form directly. An agent will reach out to complete your purchase face to face. No payment happens on this website.

What's the difference between MineBig 4D and 6D?

MineBig 4D uses a 4-digit number; MineBig 6D uses a 6-digit number. Both draw live every Sunday at 12 PM, each with its own prizes.

Draws & Results

When are the draws held?

Every Sunday at 12:00 PM (your local time). The live event runs for 15 minutes — watch it on the Next Draw page, which shows countdowns for both games.

How do I check draw results?

Three ways: watch the live draw, visit Results & Winners for the latest numbers and past draws, or check a specific ticket on the Check page.

How are winning numbers generated?

Every MineBig draw follows a strict, transparent process so every number has an equal chance of winning. [Exact draw mechanism — officiation, generation and verification — is confirmed with the client before launch.]

Prizes & Claiming

What prizes are on offer?

Five tiers per game: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, Special and Consolation — for both MineBig 4D and MineBig 6D. Prize amounts are announced with each draw (amounts pending client confirmation).

How do I claim a prize?

Face to face, from your agent — the same person who sold you the ticket. Your agent confirms your number, hands over the prize, and that's it. No apps, no hidden steps.

Are winner names published?

No — results show the winning number and the winner's city and country only. Winner identity stays between the winner and their agent.

Account & Support

How do I log in?

Use the Log In button in the header. One login, two channels: choose Player (track your numbers) or Agent (sell and lock numbers, keep a permanent log book).

Is MineBig safe and legitimate?

The draw is live and open for everyone to watch, results are published transparently, and every sale and prize goes through verified agents with a permanent record. Please also read our Play Responsibly page.

Who do I contact for help?

Reach our team via the Contact Us page — phone, email, or the message form. Customer service is available 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM.
