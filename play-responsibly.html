Play Responsibly

We want every MineBig draw to stay what it is meant to be: a little hope, a little fun, once a week. Please keep these commitments in mind:

  • Play for fun, not as an income. Treat tickets as entertainment — never as a way to make money or solve money problems.
  • Set your own limits. Decide ahead of time what you're comfortable spending, and stick to it.
  • Ask for help when it stops being fun. If playing ever feels stressful, compulsive, or out of control, talk to someone you trust and consider professional support.
  • Agents are trained to care. Our agents are instructed to support responsible play — you can always say no, and a responsible agent will respect it.

If you or someone you know needs help with gambling, reach out to a local support service in Malaysia, such as Befrienders Malaysia (03-7956 8145) or a licensed counseling service near you.

Privacy

This is a demo prototype. The preview stores sample data (such as names, codes and emails you type) only in your own browser's local storage on your own device — nothing is transmitted to any server, and nothing is shared with anyone.

  • Data you enter in this demo (codes, lead form, newsletter) stays on your device and can be cleared by clearing your browser data.
  • At launch, MineBig will publish a full privacy policy covering how player and agent information is collected, used, and protected.
  • Buyer details recorded by agents in the portal exist to deliver prizes — they are never published on the public site.

Terms & Disclaimer

  • Demo status: This website is a functional prototype for preview and feedback. It is not the live MineBig product, and nothing on it constitutes an offer to sell, a lottery entry, or a financial transaction.
  • No online transactions: MineBig.com does not process payments, sell tickets online, or collect prizes online. Tickets, receipts and prizes are handled face to face by verified agents.
  • Results: Draw results shown in this demo are sample data. Live results are published after each Sunday draw.
  • Compliance: The operating entity will comply with all applicable laws and licensing requirements in every region it operates in before going live.
  • Fair play: One locked code per combination per week, transparent published results, and a permanent agent log book — that is the promise of Version 2.0.
